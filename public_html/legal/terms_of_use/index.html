How doremi.chat works

doremi.chat works inside messaging apps you already use: WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, Discord and email. You talk to it by message, and it answers in the same chat.

As you chat, doremi.chat looks for people who share your interests. That search is your discovery. It can lead you to channels: groups around a shared interest, made by doremi.chat. A channel has 5 seats, and each person in it gets a free name: do, re, mi, fa or sol. That name is how the others in the channel see you. With vip you can also take a 6th seat (la) in a full channel, and with red a 6th or 7th seat (la or si).

You can also create parties (your own groups), add contacts and use the other commands listed on the doremi.chat page.

WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram only let us message you for 24 hours after your last message. Send / to keep the chat open.

AI features
